BIO

I've been a professional photographer for 6 years, working with couples, small businesses, and lifestyle brands across the region. I specialize in natural light photography and love turning ordinary moments into something people want to look back on for years.

EXPERIENCE

Over the past 6 years, I've shot over 150 weddings and events, and collaborated with more than 30 small businesses on product and brand photography. My work has been featured in two local lifestyle magazines, and I've built long-term relationships with several clients who return every year for family portraits and business shoots. I approach every project the same way — get to know the people or brand first, then let that understanding guide how I shoot.
